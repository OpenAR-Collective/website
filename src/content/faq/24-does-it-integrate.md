group: "The software"

That is the goal, and the platform is being designed from the start to make integration straightforward. The platform exposes a documented, versioned API, a defined set of technical entry points that other systems can connect to, which means any vendor with the technical capability can build an integration. Common AR industry integrations (credit reporting, skip tracing, payment processing, letter vendors, dialer systems, scoring services, and so on) are exactly the kinds of connections the platform's module system is built to support.

In the early days, the available integrations will be limited to what the Foundation, its contributors, and early-adopting vendors have built. Over time, as the platform's user base grows, vendors will have a commercial incentive to build and maintain their own integrations the same way they do for the major commercial AR platforms today. The Foundation will publish integration standards and certification criteria, so agencies can have confidence that an integration meets quality and security expectations. The model is the same one that has produced rich integration ecosystems around every major open-source platform: open standards, public documentation, and a competitive vendor ecosystem.
