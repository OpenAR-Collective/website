---
question: "Is it in the cloud (SaaS)?"
section: "The platform"
order: 22
---
No, not in the way that term is typically used. The platform is designed to be self-hosted, meaning each agency installs and runs its own copy on infrastructure it controls. That infrastructure can be cloud-based (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, or any other cloud provider) or on-premises servers in your own data center. The choice is yours. What the Collective will not do is run a shared, centralized hosted service where every agency's data lives on Foundation-controlled servers.

This is a deliberate design choice. Operating a hosted service for an entire industry would require the Foundation to handle other agencies' consumer data at massive scale, with all the regulatory liability, security obligations, and ongoing operational cost that entails. That is not the Foundation's mission. By keeping the software self-hosted, agencies maintain direct control of their data, their infrastructure, and their compliance posture, which is what most regulated organizations want anyway. Agencies that prefer not to operate the software themselves will be able to engage certified vendors who offer hosted deployments as a commercial service.
