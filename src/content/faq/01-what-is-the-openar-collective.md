question: "What is the OpenAR Collective?"
section: "The basics"
order: 1
---
The OpenAR Collective (legal name: The Open Accounts Receivable Collective Foundation) is a nonprofit organization created to support the accounts receivable and debt collection industry. Its purpose is to provide free, openly available resources to anyone working in or with the industry, including software, educational content, peer community, and compliance reference materials. Everything the Collective produces will be free and available to anyone, with no membership requirement and no commercial agenda.

The most ambitious thing the Collective is building is a free, open-source AR and collections platform: a complete software system that any agency could download and use as an alternative to the expensive commercial platforms that dominate the market today. That platform is the flagship project, but it is one piece of a broader mission to provide neutral, public-interest infrastructure for an industry that has never had it.
