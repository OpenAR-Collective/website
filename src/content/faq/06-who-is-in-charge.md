The Collective is governed by a Board of Directors, not by the founder personally. The Board is responsible for the Foundation's strategic direction, financial stewardship, and adherence to its mission. Directors are recruited for their expertise, including legal and compliance, financial, consumer advocacy, academic, and fundraising backgrounds, and serve in a fiduciary capacity, meaning they are legally obligated to act in the Foundation's best interest, not their own or any outside party's.

The Board's composition is deliberately weighted toward independent directors, meaning people with no business relationship to the founder and no financial stake in the Foundation's activities. This independence is what allows the Foundation to credibly claim neutrality. The Board, in turn, answers to the public the Foundation serves, to the IRS (which oversees nonprofit compliance), and to the state of Delaware (where the Foundation is incorporated). All of these oversight mechanisms are standard for charitable organizations and are designed to ensure the Foundation does what it says it will do.
