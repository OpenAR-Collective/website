The source code will be published on the Foundation's [GitHub organization](https://github.com/OpenAR-Collective), under the Apache License, Version 2.0. Nothing is available to download yet. The platform is in its initial architecture and design phase, and there is no release today.

Development happens in the open rather than behind a curtain, so repositories become public as they are established rather than at a later unveiling. Watching the GitHub organization is the most direct way to follow the work as it lands, and the Foundation will announce releases through its own channels.
