---
question: "Who supports it when something breaks at 2 a.m.?"
section: "How this works"
order: 8
---
This is one of the most important questions, and the honest answer has two parts.

The Collective itself does not provide 24/7 emergency support for individual users. That is not a service a small nonprofit can responsibly promise, and it is not the Collective's role. The Collective maintains the software, fixes bugs, publishes updates, and runs a community where users can ask questions and get answers from peers and contributors. Agencies that need formal support, including emergency response, integration help, and customization, will be able to engage certified vendors who provide those services commercially. The Collective will run a vendor certification program to identify firms that have demonstrated competence with the platform. This is the same model that has worked for decades in the broader open-source world: the software is free, and a competitive ecosystem of service providers offers paid help to organizations that want it. You get the cost benefits of free software and the operational reliability of professional support, with the added benefit of being able to switch service providers without switching software.
