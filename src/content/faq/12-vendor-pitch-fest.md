---
question: "How do you keep this from becoming a vendor pitch fest or a venue for sales pressure?"
group: "The Foundation"
section: "The community"
order: 12
---
Through clear community standards, active moderation, and a structural commitment to non-commercial discussion. Vendors are welcome as participants, but commercial solicitation between members is prohibited. There are no sponsored channels, no paid placements, and no opportunities for vendors to buy visibility. Moderators are empowered to enforce these standards, and because the Foundation has no commercial constituency to placate and no revenue stream tied to vendor participation, the standards can be enforced rather than quietly bent.
