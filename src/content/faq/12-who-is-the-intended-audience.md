---
question: "Who is the intended audience?"
section: "Who this is for"
order: 12
---
Everyone who works in, with, or around the accounts receivable and debt collection industry. That includes:

- Collection agencies of every size, from solo practitioners to large enterprises
- Creditors and AR departments at banks, healthcare systems, utilities, and other originators of debt
- Debt buyers and the firms that work with them
- Collection law firms
- Vendors and service providers
- Compliance professionals and consultants
- Consumer advocates and legal aid attorneys
- Academic researchers studying the industry

The Collective is deliberately broad in its audience because the resources it produces are useful across the industry. A small agency might use the software directly. A creditor might use the educational resources to better understand its vendors. A consumer advocate might use the published compliance materials to evaluate industry practices. A researcher might use anonymized industry data to study consumer outcomes. All of these audiences benefit from a neutral, public-interest organization that does not have a commercial agenda.
