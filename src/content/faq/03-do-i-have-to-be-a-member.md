question: "Do I have to be a member, pay dues, or join anything to use it?"
group: "The Foundation"
section: "About the Foundation"
order: 3
---
No. The Collective has no membership fees and no membership requirement to access anything it produces. The software, the documentation, the educational resources, the webinars, and the community are all free and open to anyone.

The Collective does offer a free membership program that provides access to the verified practitioner areas of its community. Verification confirms only that you work in or with the industry. There is no fee, no application review beyond confirming your professional engagement, and no requirement that you agree with any particular position or affiliate with any particular organization. The point of the verification is to keep peer discussion professional, not to gatekeep access.
