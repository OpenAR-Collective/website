question: "Can I promote the Collective, or do the trademark terms forbid it?"
group: "The Foundation"

Promote away. The same terms that carry the trademark language grant every member the right to say so publicly: "[Your name] is a member of the OpenAR Collective" in a LinkedIn profile, a biography, a presentation, or anywhere else, along with your member number and a member badge made for exactly that purpose. Telling colleagues and clients about the Collective, sharing what we publish, and bringing people in is not just permitted, it is how a community with no advertising budget grows. Everything the Foundation publishes is free to everyone, including creditors and first-party teams that never place accounts with an agency, so there is no wrong audience to share it with.

What the trademark language forbids is something different: making the Foundation's name part of your own brand rather than a statement about it. Naming a company or product "OpenAR Something", registering an OpenAR domain, or running a social media handle that looks like the Foundation all need written permission, because they make you look like us rather than a supporter of us. The name has to keep meaning the neutral nonprofit, and that is what makes displaying it worth anything. The full rules, including how to ask for permission for a use beyond the granted ones, are in the [Trademark Policy](/trademark).
