---
question: "If the software is free, where does the money come from?"
section: "How this works"
order: 6
---
The Collective is funded the same way other successful open-source nonprofits are funded: through a combination of charitable donations, sponsorships from organizations that benefit from the work, grants, and limited revenue from activities that do not compromise the Collective's neutrality, such as a vendor certification program. The founder is also contributing significant time and personal funding during the early years.

Comparable models exist and have worked for decades. The Linux Foundation, the Mozilla Foundation (which makes Firefox), the Python Software Foundation, the Apache Software Foundation, and the Eclipse Foundation (which stewards a large family of enterprise development tools) all operate on similar principles. The accounts receivable industry has never had an organization like this, but the funding model is well-established in the broader software world.
