---
question: "What is open-source software, in plain language?"
section: "The basics"
order: 2
---
Open-source software is software whose source code, the human-readable instructions that tell the computer what to do, is published publicly for anyone to read, copy, modify, and use. There is no licensing fee, no per-seat cost, and no vendor relationship required. You can download it, run it, and change it to fit your needs.

Most people use open-source software every day without realizing it. The Linux operating system that powers the majority of the internet's servers is open-source. The Firefox web browser is open-source. Most modern programming languages are open-source. WordPress, which runs roughly 40 percent of the world's websites, is open-source. The model is mature, well-understood, and proven across decades of use in nearly every industry.
