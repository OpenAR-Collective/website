---
question: "Why is this set up as a nonprofit instead of a regular company?"
section: "Why a nonprofit"
order: 15
---
A nonprofit is the right structure for a project that exists to serve the public rather than to generate returns for owners or investors. The Collective has no shareholders, no equity, and no profit motive. Everything it earns goes back into the mission. Its only obligation is to the public benefit it was organized to serve.

This matters for a project meant to be neutral, durable infrastructure for an entire industry. A for-profit company stewarding open-source software has an inherent conflict between what's best for the software's users and what's best for its shareholders. A nonprofit has no such conflict. The Collective is legally bound, through its incorporation documents and its nonprofit charter, to operate exclusively in the public interest. That structural commitment is exactly what makes the Collective trustworthy as a neutral host for shared industry infrastructure.
