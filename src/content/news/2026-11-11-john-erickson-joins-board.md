**FORT WORTH, Texas, November 11, 2026.** The Open Accounts Receivable Collective Foundation, operating as The OpenAR Collective, announced today that John Erickson was seated on its Board of Directors on October 27, 2026. He is the sixth director to serve on the Board, which may expand to as many as nine seats.

Erickson is the CEO of Credit Service Intl., a nationwide accounts receivable management company he has led since 2011. He also leads the company's M&A team, which has completed more than 15 agency transactions. Over more than 20 years in the industry, he has become a familiar voice on the future of collections. He is Editor-in-Residence at R.O. Hammer Companies, a content platform for professional service providers, where he interviews industry leaders on the company's podcast, and he regularly moderates and speaks on panels at industry events such as ARMTech and Brainstorm.

## The Board of Directors

The Board now has six directors, each of whom is introduced in full on the [About page](https://openarcollective.org/about/#board).

- **Rob Grafrath**, Chair
- **Mark Cavin**, Vice Chair
- **Porter Heath Morgan**, Secretary
- **Donna Weaver**, Treasurer
- **Lucas Brown**, Director
- **John Erickson**, Director

The Foundation intends to fill its remaining seats from people who are actively contributing to the community, drawn from a range of backgrounds and perspectives rather than from a single corner of the industry. Seats are not available through sponsorship or donation, and the Foundation's Anti-Capture Policy limits any single employer or commonly controlled group to one affiliated director.

## About The OpenAR Collective

The Open Accounts Receivable Collective Foundation, operating under the trade name The OpenAR Collective, is a Delaware nonprofit corporation working to make accounts receivable and debt collection more transparent, more compliant, and more accountable to the consumers it touches. It builds software under an open-source license so anyone can run, inspect, modify, and redistribute it at no cost. The Foundation also publishes open educational and compliance resources, develops shared standards, delivers training, and maintains a neutral, community-governed commons where practitioners learn from one another. More information is available at [openarcollective.org](https://openarcollective.org).
